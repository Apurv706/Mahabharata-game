## Built 29 Oct
1. **Ugra's wrestling bout** plays on the empty fair ring (`fight_fair_ring`).
2. **Drona is selective.** Court children (Hastinapura, or Kuru standing 5) ask as before. Everyone else must first hit Drona's bird on the highest branch, 2 of 3 arrows; children of charioteers and Nishadas need 3 of 3. Pass: he takes you grudgingly (Kuru standing +2). Fail: choose another teacher.
3. **Choose, then prove it.** The path choice is now the armoury: seven things laid out, each saying whether it suits your best attribute ("Your might suits the mace", "Your aim suits the bow"). The path's test follows at once; fail twice and you choose again. Owner confirmed keeping this form.
4. **Archery aim and placement.** You stand in the lower-left third facing the target; teacher and student apart; the aim runs from your bow hand; the bow arm follows the target.
8. **Puppets on the ground** in fights, with foot shadows and a screen check.
9. **Moves that cannot be used** are tucked into one quiet line.
10. **The lac house** opens calm; the fire clip plays when the house burns.
11. **The stealth guard** carries the torch and patrols the far side, with a tell and a lock-on.
12. **The map follows the story**, checked by the playthrough test.
13. **Commander test, "Set the formation"**; a company fights beside its commander.
14. **Chariot driver's run** on the eve of the war.
15. **The last stand in the wheel** (Pandava side).
16. **Divine weapons look divine**; the counter-weapon clash against heroes.
17. **Kaurava side of the wheel**: stand in Abhimanyu's way and fall; "Call for help"; the astra listed for all.
18. **Sound**: call to arms, death sounds, music under tests, a piece for each of 16 places. All synthesised in code.

20. **The move to Hastinapura** plays on the map after the scene that tells why the family was called there.
21. **Karna's horse**: a leather puppet horse in place of the glow.
6–7. (Earlier) choices that went nowhere now play out; second chances open on their test's painting.

## Built 30 Oct (decided in the 30 Oct design session)
22. **Moments: friendship, divine weapons and big milestones.** A friend for life, a divine weapon, your teacher's yes, your marriage, a vow, a company of your own: a few seconds full screen (a painting moving slowly, a gold title, one line, a synthesised theme; tap to go on). Friends' figures step in from the side edges and reach for each other's hands; the astra's arrow of light comes down into your hand and its glow stays. A friendship that ends gets a grey farewell. Kept as cards in the character sheet and the Book of Lives. Option B art installed (five friendships, the astra, the teacher, the marriage, tall and wide; portraits of Yudhishthira and Vidura); the repaints arrived and were installed on 31 Oct.
23. **The practice ground (akhara), option 1: no reward.** Reached from the menu between scenes, beside a second chance ("Practise first, then try again"), and after a lost fight that was not deadly. Every kind of test met in this life (and in earlier lives) can be rehearsed with its real game; kinds not met stay locked. Sparring under the real fight rules against a straw dummy, a fellow student or the teacher (never the opponent who beat you), with no death and nothing recorded; a drill for reading blows, slow then faster. The teacher's figure gives a tip for each kind and a "Show me"; three speeds; a calm tabla drill. Everything runs on a copy of the life, put back exactly afterwards; never inside a deadly fight, a perilous choice or a timed moment.
24. **Vows that matter.** Each vow's gift, watchers and price, shown when you swear it and on any choice that would break it.
    - Gifts: never to retreat +1 attack and one more after holding firm in a deadly fight; never to lie, "My word" (once an act a statecraft test passes without a roll) and +1 on honest persuasion; never to strike the unarmed, +1 stealth and a spared foe who drags you out of a deadly fight once; never to strike Karna, Karna answers your call across the lines; celibacy, +1 Wisdom and Bhishma's blessing (+1 defence in the war); vengeance, +1 attack against the sworn foe.
    - Prices: watchers' bonds −3; the company shaken in its next fight; the chariot wheel sticks once in a war fight; a weapon given on that condition lost; Karna's friendship ended; the blessing and the Wisdom point lost; an unanswered vengeance darkens the telling.
    - **A friendship below 3 ends** (said on the choice before you make it): a farewell with the friend's last words; a call for help no longer reaches them. Every deadly war fight has "Call for help". A friend you married does not walk away.
    - **Weapon conditions:** Drona and the voice in the fire (penance), never against someone who cannot fight back; Kripa, never lie; Mauni, never say who taught you.
    - **Friends who pull at a vow:** lie for your friend at court (Act III); fall back at a river crossing to carry your wounded friend (Act V); silence a bound spy (Act V). Each has an honest third way through a test.
    - **The way back:** one heavy penance a life lifts a broken vow's price; a lost friend comes back only by riding to find them.
25. **Archery: what you see is what counts.** The target is drawn exactly as wide as its hit zone and a shot crosses exactly where it is judged; a target too small to see at its true size is drawn bigger with a small gold mark that is the whole hit zone; the first test is a little gentler; a thrown stone knocks the fruit down. Checked over about 230 automated shots.

## Built 31 Oct (the rest of PRD §18, for phone and web)
26. **Friends in any fight** (§18.1, §18.9). A friend at bond 7 (5 for a friend for life) comes when called, unless dead, estranged, on the other side or away in the story; a friend left badly hurt carries a scar and remembers it; Charu, who wears no armour, can die in a deadly fight you called them into. Not in a teacher's sparring bout. The puppets gain a moving jaw, a turn to flee, distance and lamplight, an elder's stoop, a quiver and a satchel; "Meet blows for me" (on by default in story mode). Practice ground: speed and help levels for board games; tests met in earlier lives stay unlocked.
27. **The night the lac house burns** (§18.2). Those who know the plot can cut the oil lines in the cellar or warn Yudhishthira; everyone runs the burning house (a 5×5 plan, fire spreading from Purochana's corner, smoke, stuck doors, carrying someone out). Late, burnt, or dead in the fire ("The burnt house" ending; burns in story mode). Canon holds.
28. **Rising to command** (§18.3). Three ranks; the army battle (place companies on the day's formation, then give orders); four holding fights against Karna's and Arjuna's chariots, Duryodhana's line and Bhima's charge, won by lasting; a broken line costs the next deadly fight; three war councils.
29. **Story threads and episodes** (§18.4–18.6). Ugra across a life; whom you tell Karna's secret; a wrong ask from Vidura, Kripa, Balarama or Mauni; an elder brother or sister on the other side. The exile storm, Charu at the Yaksha's lake, the false name and Kichaka's hall, the supply carts, false orders, the truce at dusk, Shikhandi's charge, and four after-war scenes (rebuild, pilgrimage, the river rite, a student of your own). Bards, letters across the lines, keepsakes.
30. **Divine weapons, shields, magic and tapas** (§18.7–18.8). Five named astras, each with its own quest and condition (at most two offered a life), chosen in a fight from one "Invoke astra" card; the charmed shield, the kavacha and the raksha sutra; Bala and Atibala, the sanjivani root, Antardhana, an elder's blessing, Soma, the rite to lift a curse; tapas (0–20) from kept vows, celibacy and penance, halved by a broken vow; the Brahmastra temptation, always refusable.
31. **Fixes found in testing.**
    - On small phones, fights with a divine weapon keep "Call for help" and "Withdraw" on screen (shorter move cards with seven moves).
    - The painting's ground follows the fighters whenever the moves panel changes (the weapon chooser; Safari's sparring and two-weapon fights on tall screens).
    - The dive shows only the diver.
    - Fights with a friend's card fit wide screens and short phones.
    - The dark band behind tests on desktop, and the stretched Saving column in the menu.
    - The string collector and the playthrough test no longer run out of memory.
    - Every new line has its Hindi.
    - Puppets checked standing on the painted ground in every test and fight, on every phone, tablet and desktop size.
