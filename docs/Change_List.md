Fail: choose another teacher.

4. **Archery aim and placement.** You stand in the lower-left third facing the target; teacher and student apart; the aim runs from your bow hand; the bow arm follows the target.

14. **Chariot driver's run** on the eve of the war.

## Built 30 Oct (decided in the 30 Oct design session)
22. **Moments: friendship, divine weapons and big milestones.** A friend for life, a divine weapon, your teacher's yes, your marriage, a vow, a company of your own: a few seconds full screen (a painting moving slowly, a gold title, one line, a synthesised theme; tap to go on). Friends' figures step in from the side edges and reach for each other's hands; the astra's arrow of light comes down into your hand and its glow stays. A friendship that ends gets a grey farewell. Kept as cards in the character sheet and the Book of Lives. Option B art installed (five friendships, the astra, the teacher, the marriage, tall and wide; portraits of Yudhishthira and Vidura); the repaints arrived and were installed on 31 Oct.
23. **The practice ground (akhara), option 1: no reward.** Reached from the menu between scenes, beside a second chance ("Practise first, then try again"), and after a lost fight that was not deadly. Every kind of test met in this life (and in earlier lives) can be rehearsed with its real game; kinds not met stay locked. Sparring under the real fight rules against a straw dummy, a fellow student or the teacher (never the opponent who beat you), with no death and nothing recorded; a drill for reading blows, slow then faster. The teacher's figure gives a tip for each kind and a "Show me"; three speeds; a calm tabla drill. Everything runs on a copy of the life, put back exactly afterwards; never inside a deadly fight, a perilous choice or a timed moment.
24. **Vows that matter.** Each vow's gift, watchers and price, shown when you swear it and on any choice that would break it.
    - Gifts: never to retreat +1 attack and one more after holding firm in a deadly fight; never to lie, "My word" (once an act a statecraft test passes without a roll) and +1 on honest persuasion; never to strike the unarmed, +1 stealth and a spared foe who drags you out of a deadly fight once; never to strike Karna, Karna answers your call across the lines; celibacy, +1 Wisdom and Bhishma's blessing (+1 defence in the war); vengeance, +1 attack against the sworn foe.
    - Prices: watchers' bonds −3; the company shaken in its next fight; the chariot wheel sticks once in a war fight; a weapon given on that condition lost; Karna's friendship ended; the blessing and the Wisdom point lost; an unanswered vengeance darkens the telling.
    - **A friendship below 3 ends** (said on the choice before you make it): a farewell with the friend's last words; a call for help no longer reaches them. Every deadly war fight has "Call for help". A friend you married does not walk away.
    - **Weapon conditions:** Drona and the voice in the fire (penance), never against someone who cannot fight back; Kripa, never lie; Mauni, never say who taught you.
    - **Friends who pull at a vow:** lie for your friend at court (Act III); fall back at a river crossing to carry your wounded friend (Act V); silence a bound spy (Act V). Each has an honest third way through a test.
    - **The way back:** one heavy penance a life lifts a broken vow's price; a lost friend comes back only by riding to find them.
25. **Archery: what you see is what counts.** The target is drawn exactly as wide as its hit zone and a shot crosses exactly where it is judged; a target too small to see at its true size is drawn bigger with a small gold mark that is the whole hit zone; the first test is a little gentler; a thrown stone knocks the fruit down. Checked over about 230 automated shots.

## Built 31 Oct (the rest of PRD §18, for phone and web)

## Fixed 1 Nov (code review)

    - **Keys and the lamp:** number keys no longer reach the hidden screen during title cards, and a held key doesn't repeat. The timed lamp never returns after a result.
    - **Soma and Bala** no longer cost a point for good.
    - **Story:**
      - six scenes are no longer lost in about 30% of lives;
      - the hidden year plays only for those in Matsya;
      - Ugra fights on one side only;
      - Vasu's fate is consistent;
      - the wheel test comes before Abhimanyu's death;
      - vengeance on Ashwatthama can be sworn;
      - the sibling's {who} is filled;
      - refusing the night raid has its own line;
      - a dead Charu no longer acts.
    - **Hindi tools** can no longer scramble or drop translations.
    - **Tests:** a new engine test (`tests/lives.js`) and a GitHub "Engine checks" run on every push.

## Built 2 Nov: a faster start
37. **A prologue at Kurukshetra.**
    - **Where it comes:** a new life opens on the thirteenth day of the war, before the childhood. It is marked as a dream ("None of this has happened yet"; the header reads "Years from now").
    - **The fight:** you are grown, with a sword. You fight two soldiers and a warrior in a dented helmet whose face you cannot see, for three rounds.
    - **The cut:** "The dream breaks." The screen goes white: "The field is thirty-eight years away. You are six, and you are waking." Then the Act I card. Your earliest memory opens with "You wake with dust in your mouth that is not there."
    - **Kept apart from the life:** not on your record, not on the map, no act card of its own.
38. **Stakes before age 8:**
    - **The game of tag by the lotus pond** is now a stealth test played on the lotus garden.
      - On Bhima's side you creep up on Duryodhana: tag him and Bhima throws him in the pond (he remembers).
      - On Duryodhana's side Bhima hunts you: escape and Duryodhana calls you "one of us"; get caught and you are held under.
      - Both outcomes keep the way to that prince as a friend at 10.
    - **The sage on the road names the war:**
      - Fed, he says "Keep your feet for eighteen days".
      - Mocked, he curses you "on the field where eighteen armies die" (his curse does fall in the war's fights).
      - Passed by, he says "eighteen" to the empty road.
39. **Ugra and Charu at seven.**
    - **The new scene, "The washing":** Ugra tips your washing into the river, and a child you have never spoken to jumps in after it. You choose: go for Ugra, jump in and help, or threaten him with your father.
    - **Later payoffs:** at 10 Charu is the one who jumped into the river; at 13 Ugra is the boy who tipped your washing, and if you fought him he grins: "He has not forgotten the river steps either."
40. **The quill:** tried writing only the important lines (2 Nov), and put back the same day. It writes every line again.
41. **Fixed from the first tester's pass:**
    - **Switching to Hindi or English mid-test** left the test's title, score line, button and help in the old language. They now follow the switch in place, and the test goes on.
    - **Single-action screens** (Begin before a test, Continue after a fight, Live another life) only answered the number 1. They now answer Enter too.
42. **Fixed from the tester's later passes:**
    - **Fights that seemed frozen:**
      - If your moves are on screen while enemies are still due to act, their turns now carry on.
      - A stunned enemy's lost turn restarts if a redraw interrupts it.
      - A tap on a move is never silently ignored: the fight redraws to show whose turn it really is.
      - A round whose last foes have fallen closes itself.
    - **Age never goes backward.**
      - The light on the mountain (now 36), the river vow scene (42) and Vidura's scene (29) carry ages in story order.
      - The engine never makes you younger, and Continue keeps your age. The one exception is waking from the prologue's dream.
    - **The false-name test at Virata's court is a lie:**
      - A vow never to lie is warned of on the choice and broken by it.
      - Vow-holders can instead answer with only true things and let the steward draw the wrong conclusion (a statecraft test).
43. **Polish from the same tester:**

    - **Ugra is reintroduced** as "the miller's son from the spring fair" at the tournament, in his letter, on the first day of the war and on the seventeenth day.

44. **English text checked against the humanizer patterns.**
    - **Already clean:** no dashes, no "not X but Y" staging, no inflated words.
    - **Repeated beats cut back:**
      - "…, which is worse" from 10 endings to 3.

45. **Into the dream: the opening sequence** (about thirteen seconds, or a tap):
    1. Your homeland's painting fills the screen and is introduced, as on the character screen: where it lies, its name, and what it is.
    2. You, six years old, walk home along the bank and in at your lit doorway.
    3. Supper with the family: grandmother, mother, father and you, sitting on a mat with leaf plates.
    4. Bed on a rope charpai, the clay lamp burning low and going out.
    5. Dream bubbles rise from the sleeper, and the war opens out of the last one.
    6. **Waking:** the dream fight ends straight in white, with no result panel ("The field goes white. Far away, someone is calling your name."), which fades into the dark room:
       - You sit up on the bed, shaking, sweat running.
       - Your mother comes in with a lamp and kneels by you: "Hush. It was only a dream. You are home, and I am here."
       - Your father, in the doorway: "The sages have said it for years: the Kuru cousins will go to war, and the whole land with them. Perhaps you saw it. Then grow strong, and be ready when it comes."
       - Then Act I. A tap moves on a beat.
    - **The dream page is cut down** to one line of context ("The thirteenth day of the great war at Kurukshetra: the five Pandava brothers against their hundred Kaurava cousins") and one of action.
    - Captions are in English and Hindi. There is a new "sit" pose for the meal.
    - `Art_Brief_Opening.md` asks for two empty rooms (supper and night) to replace the plain wall. Each homeland's house at dusk is optional.
    - **Your age stays six in the dream.** Only the figure is grown, with a sword; the Character panel no longer says 44.
    - **The story sheet reaches the bottom of iPhone screens again:** newer Safari floats its address bar over the page, which left a band of painting under the sheet.
    - **Later the same day:** supper has a second line on what time with the family is like ("Grandmother's old stories, your father's bad jokes, and always a little more rice for you."); on waking, the mother kneels by the bed with her hand out and sets the lamp down behind her, and the floating sweat drops are gone.
    - **From the tester's third life:** a journal line is written only once per life (the spy path's appeared twice); word tests say "best of 3", since two right or two wrong settle them early. The automated playthroughs no longer stop at the opening fight.

## Built 2 Nov: the quality review (branch `quality-review`, on the private play page for review; not on the public site until approved)
Following `Quality_Review_2026-11-02.md` and its "Decided 2 November" section. The backlit-leather look (items 1–3) was rejected there and is not built.
46. **Staging (items 8–12):** far enemies are solid (no blur or see-through); a soft shadow under every figure in fights and tests; fight figures larger; in tests the figures stand above the line of help; each test gives the right gear (a bow to shoot; empty hands to wrestle, hold steady or creep); a push test with no one named gets an opponent.
47. **Puppets with weight (items 13–15):** a new `js/ui/puppet-anim.js` drives each joint from a spring, timed by the clock so it moves the same on any screen. Limbs overshoot and settle; a blow has a wind-up, a short freeze on contact, knockback and flakes of leather; the dead fall limp from the feet. With reduced motion the old poses are used.
48. **Fight moves (items 17–19):** the bow is held upright with a string that draws to the face; an archer's Strike is a quick shot; Aimed shot is drawn and held with a trembling arm; Volley arcs three arrows; Pinning shot leaves the arrow in the ground at the foe's foot while he strains; Guard throws sparks and shows the halved number; Healing herbs kneels in green light. Friends come in from the edge of the stage, speaking. The enemy's next blow waits until your move has played out. On phones the moves are a strip of three across, names only (the description is the card's tooltip).
