# Art Brief — *Kurukshetra: A Life*

Companion to the PRD (§12 cultural rules, §14 art direction).

## Rules for every image

- **No painted text.** Titles, captions and stats belong in the game's own layout so they can be translated and stay sharp.
- Main subject in the centre; phones crop the sides.
- A calmer lower third, where text sits over the image.
- JPG or WebP, ideally under 500 KB.
- Rights: only work you made, commissioned or licensed, or that is public domain. No TV-serial stills, no Amar Chitra Katha panels. Disclose AI-generated art on Steam.

## Content rules (PRD §12)

- Krishna and divine figures: keep out for now; later, only with dignity, never as a costume or action pose.
- Draupadi in the dice game: show the hall, the dice and the court's reaction, never her humiliation.
- War: violence implied, no gore (Teen/16+).
- Canon character portraits need advisory review before public use.
- Architecture: early historic India (timber, brick and stone towns, gateways, courtyards, stupas). Avoid domes, Gothic spires, castle towers and later temple spires.
