# Human-paced playtest, 5 November 2026

Five lives played through the built play pages (Part One, then Part Two) with real taps on phones and clicks and keys on a laptop. The pace was a person's: actions about 1.6× slower than the test bot, and a reading pause on every page in proportion to its length. Each life had its own homeland and weapon. The tool is `tests/review_play.js --human --land … --path …`, which audits each page for text covering over half the screen and controls off the screen, and each real-time blow for how far apart the two fighters stood against the weapon's reach.

## Bugs and problems

1. **The lac-house escape killed 3 of the 4 lives without story mode**, on both tries and again after going back to Act III. It failed on an iPhone 15 twice and on a laptop once; the iPhone SE life got out.
   - The fire spreads one room every 1.4–1.75 s. A move takes 0.35 s, 0.55 s in smoke, and a stuck door takes a 0.8 s hold.
   - On the laptop, the test player presses an arrow key once at a stuck door. If a held key is needed there, the death there may be the test player's, not the game's.
   - The phone deaths happened with the door held. *To check by hand* before tuning. A player losing half their lives before Act IV is a problem either way.
2. **You can stand on top of a foe in the next row** (real time). A foe in your own row blocks your walk, but nothing stops you walking into the same spot as a foe one row over and striking from there.
   - In the cattle raid with Karna (life 3), about 100 of your blows landed at 6–19% of the weapon's reach apart.
   - In the Gandharva ambush, down to 5%.
   - Foes are already kept from standing stacked on you from another row; the player needs the same rule.
3. **Foes strike from inside your weapon's reach** (real time). In the Gandharva ambush (life 2, mace), 30 blows landed at 41–75% of the reach. The sparring bout (`e_spar`, life 1) had 40 blows at 51–78%. The border spy fight had 3 at 71–77%. In the sparring bout the fighters use wooden swords, so the reach may be taken from the path's own weapon.
4. **War day 1 lost twice in 4 rounds** (life 2, mace, iPhone SE). Deadly war days have no Try again (owner, 5 November), so one bad day 1 ends the life. Either day 1 is too hard for a mace fighter or the test player's war-day choices are weak. Check against the balance numbers for `e_day1`.
5. **The archer's path test failed four times** in life 1 before the life moved on. At a person's pace the early tests may be too hard.
6. **One Gandharva blow's tip missed the body** it was counted against (life 3).
