# Kurukshetra: A Life - Batch: 3 Mathura paintings (remaining art brief)

Three new paintings for Mathura, the Yadava homeland on the Yamuna under King Kamsa,
named exactly as the game loads them. Per the Oct 26 "Remaining Art Brief" (5-page version).
Mathura replaces Dwaraka as the homeland; custom_dwaraka and roots_dwaraka are no longer used.

- home_mathura.jpg    - The homeland panorama: Mathura on the Yamuna at golden hour; timber-and-brick town above wide ghats, cowherds driving cattle along the bank, Kamsa's grim fortress on the high ground
- roots_mathura.jpg   - The people you came from: a family house over the Yamuna ghats; boats arriving with cloth and spices, an uncle trading at the steps, children swimming in the shallows
- custom_mathura.jpg  - Lamps on the Yamuna: Kartika full moon; families on the ghats setting tiny leaf boats with lamps on the river, children wading to launch them

## Spec checklist (per the brief)
- 3:1 at 1800x600, JPG, each under 500 KB (370-452 KB)
- Main subject in the centre third; calm lower third for captions
- No painted text, titles, captions, labels, signatures or watermarks
- Architecture only timber, brick, stone, thatch; no domes, spires, towers or colonnades
- No blood or gore; no Krishna or other divine figures shown; all figures are generic townspeople
- No modern objects
- Each painting rendered individually at 1916x821, then cropped and downsampled to 1800x600 - never upscaled, never cut from a sheet

## AI disclosure (for Steam)
All 3 paintings in this batch are AI-generated, then individually inspected and finished (cropped/downsized) for the game.

No canon characters are shown; all figures are generic, unrecognizable people. The brief's advisory review of canon portrayals before public use still applies, as it did for earlier batches.
