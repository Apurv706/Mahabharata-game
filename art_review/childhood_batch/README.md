## Spec checklist (per the brief)
- 3:1 at 1800x600, JPG, each under 500 KB (445-498 KB)
- Main subject in the centre third; calm lower third for captions
- No painted text, titles, captions, labels, signatures or watermarks
- Architecture only timber, brick, stone, thatch; no domes, spires, towers or colonnades
- No blood or gore; no divine figures shown (the Champa shrine has an empty niche with lamps only)
- No modern objects: no bullseye targets, no saddles or stirrups, no European swords or Greek helmets
- Each painting rendered individually at full size; cropped and downsampled only, never upscaled, never cut from a sheet
