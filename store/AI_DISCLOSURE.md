# AI disclosure for Steam

Steamworks asks about AI in the content survey (Steamworks → your app → Edit Store Page → Content Survey → "AI Generation Disclosure"). There are two parts:

- **Pre-generated:** content made with AI tools during development that ships with the game or appears on the store page.
- **Live-generated:** content an AI makes while the game is running.

This game has pre-generated AI content only. The store page shows players the short text below, under "AI Generated Content Disclosure". Valve sees the long text and reviews the content the same way it reviews everything else.

Before you submit, check every line marked **[confirm]**.

---

## 1. For the store page (players see this)

> This game's paintings, its living-painting animations, its music and much of its writing were made with the help of generative AI, then chosen, edited, corrected and checked by the developer. The paintings and animations were made with AI image and video tools from the developer's own reference paintings and descriptions. The story text and the Hindi translation were drafted with an AI writing assistant and edited by the developer. The Hindi has been reviewed by native speakers **[confirm before release]**. Nothing in the game is generated by AI while you play.

## 2. For the content survey: pre-generated content (Valve sees this)

> **Visual art.** About 230 paintings and illustrations (scenes, characters, homelands, events, test and fight stages in wide and tall versions, endings, the key art and the Steam store and library images) were made with AI image generation tools **[confirm: name the tools the artists used]**. The developer wrote the briefs, the artists generated and edited the images (crops, retouches, recompositions from approved paintings, upscaling), and the developer reviewed every image before it went into the game. Images that showed the wrong period details were sent back and redone. Canon characters and sensitive scenes (the dice hall, Draupadi) are getting a separate review by a cultural adviser before release **[confirm]**.
>
> **Animation.** 25 short silent clips ("living paintings", 4 to 10 seconds each) were made with Runway Gen-4 Turbo (image to video) from the game's own approved paintings. The developer chose each clip, rejected the failed takes, trimmed two clips and encoded them for the game.
>
> **Writing.** The English story text (scenes, choices, characters, journal and interface) was drafted with an AI writing assistant (Anthropic's Claude) under the developer's direction and from the developer's design documents. The developer edited it **[confirm how much]**. The Hindi translation (about 3,300 strings) was drafted with the same assistant and is being reviewed by native Hindi speakers **[confirm]**.
>
> **Music and drawings in code.** The three music pieces and five short victory themes were composed as code by the same AI assistant, in named ragas, and rendered with a software synthesizer. The shadow-puppet figures, the map and the interface icons are vector drawings written as code by the same assistant. The developer directed and reviewed them.
>
> **Not AI:** the game's design, rules, structure and choices are the developer's. The game is based on the public-domain Sanskrit epic, the Mahabharata. It contains no voice acting, real people's likenesses or copyrighted characters.

## 3. For the content survey: live-generated content

> None. The game does not use AI while it runs. Every word, image, clip and sound is fixed when the game ships.

## Before submitting

1. Name the image tools used for the paintings (ask each artist, or check their notes in `art_review/`).
2. State how much of the story text you edited or wrote yourself.
3. Update the Hindi line once the native review is done.
4. Update the adviser line once the advisory review is done.
5. If any art or text is replaced by human-made work before release, take it out of the lists above.
